This is a modern, low-profile armchair with an integrated extending footrest, designed as a comfortable lounge chair. It features thick, blocky rectangular armrests framing a plush seat cushion and a gently reclined backrest cushion. A wide, flat, rounded-corner footrest panel extends horizontally from the front base of the chair. The entire piece is upholstered in a uniform, soft-textured fabric with an extremely pale lavender-white color.
