This sleek, rectangular kitchen island features a thick, polished white marble countertop characterized by soft, diagonal grey veining. The countertop overhangs a multi-compartment cabinet base that sits flush to the floor on a low plinth. The front and side panels of the base are wrapped in a uniform, matte, warm-grey wood veneer with a subtle vertical grain. On the opposite functional side, the island reveals seven modular bays filled with a playful mix of cabinet doors and drawers. These fronts showcase a diverse palette, including matte white, slate-purple, natural light-brown wood, and matching warm-grey wood, each accented by a small, cylindrical metal knob.
