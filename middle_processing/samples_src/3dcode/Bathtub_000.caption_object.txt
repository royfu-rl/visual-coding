This modern, minimalist freestanding bathtub features an elongated, oval-shaped basin with a clean, semi-glossy white ceramic finish. The tub's walls gently taper downward from a rounded, smooth rim to a narrower base. It sits upon a matching, low-profile solid base platform that mirrors the oval contour of the main body, raising the tub roughly 0.18 units off the floor. Inside, the spacious basin has a smooth, curved interior and features a small, circular drain hole positioned off-center along the bottom. The overall proportions of the bathtub are elongated, being nearly twice as long as it is wide, with a depth of approximately two-thirds of its width.
