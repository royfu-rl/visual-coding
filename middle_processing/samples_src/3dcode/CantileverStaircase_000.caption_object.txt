A straight, modern staircase featuring thirteen thick, rectangular treads with a dark charcoal-grey finish. The steps rise at a consistent, uniform angle, supported symmetrically on both sides by a dual-handrail system. Each handrail is crafted from a dark, rectangular-profile bar running parallel to the incline of the steps, with short, flat horizontal extensions at both the top and bottom landings. Supporting the handrails is a dense series of slender, metallic silver vertical posts, with one post positioned near the outer edge of every single tread on both sides. The entire structure exhibits crisp bilateral symmetry and clean, industrial lines with contrasting dark and light metallic elements.
