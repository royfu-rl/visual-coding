@joshpuckett

Co-founder of Iteration, a design practice partnering with select early-stage founders, and Graphical, a tool for creating visual languages. Previously, I co-founded Upperstudy, an apprenticeship based studio that helped over a hundred designers kickstart their careers in technology.

Before that, I led teams at Wealthfront, Dropbox, and Groupon.

I've been fortunate to collaborate as a designer, advisor, and investor across companies including Browserbase, Checkmate, Clubhouse, Equals, Framer, Modal, Render, Runway, Sydecar, Tia, Truework, Vanta, Vise, and many more.
