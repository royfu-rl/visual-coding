Before you ask

What is Swash?

Swash is a motion design editor that runs in your browser. Type your text, pick a preset and you have a finished animation in minutes, then add shaders, animated backgrounds, galleries of your work, widgets and sound. Everything stays editable, and you export a video. There is nothing to install, and new features ship most weeks: see what’s new.

How fast can I make something?

Most posts take a few minutes. Every preset is a complete animation, the templates open ready to remix, and changing the words, the font or the colours keeps the motion intact.

What can I make besides animated text?

Galleries that turn a folder of images or videos into a moving sequence in 21 layouts, 24 animated backgrounds, ready-made widgets such as a counter, terminal, chat, globe, map, cursor and ransom note, and 41 shader effects from grain and halftone to glitch, pixel sort and CRT. Animations can make sound too, from a library of over 270 sounds.

Is Swash free?

Yes, you can start for free. The free plan includes the full editor, up to 3 projects and export up to 720p at 30 fps with a Swash watermark, plus a few watermark-free exports each month. Pro costs $12 a month and adds unlimited projects, export up to 4K at 60 fps without a watermark and the premium presets, templates and fonts, with a 7-day free trial.

Do I need to sign up?

No. The editor opens without an account and keeps your work in the browser. Sign in to save projects to your account and to open templates from the community.

What formats can I export?

MP4 video, GIF up to 540 px, WebM with a transparent background for overlays in browsers that support it, and a single PNG frame.

Can I use my own fonts?

Yes. Upload any .ttf or .otf file, or pick from about 200 open-licensed Google fonts. Variable fonts work too, and their axes, such as weight and width, can be animated.

Is Swash an alternative to After Effects?

For text animation, social clips, motion posters and product videos, yes: you get the result in the browser in minutes, without keyframing every letter. For character animation or compositing live footage, After Effects is still the right tool.

Does Swash work with Figma?

Yes. The free Swash plugin for Figma brings your text and layout over to Swash as editable layers.
