Questions, answered

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone who creates or changes content. People who only view or comment are free on every plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Registered nonprofits and schools get Team at 50% off. Write to us with your registration number.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep read-only access for 90 days and can export everything as Markdown or PDF.
