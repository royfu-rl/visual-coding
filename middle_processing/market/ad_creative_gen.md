# AI-Generated Advertising Creatives (ad video + ad image), 国内+国外, state as of Oct 2026

Method note: about 17 web searches/fetches. Many company revenue figures come from third-party trackers (GetLatka, Sacra, ARR Club) and are labelled as estimates. Company-disclosed or earnings-call figures are labelled as such.

## Q1. Market map: sub-categories, companies, funding, traction, tech stack

### Takeaway
The segment with the most proven revenue is **avatar/UGC ad video**: HeyGen said it hit $100M ARR in Oct 2025, and Synthesia was valued at $4B in Jan 2026. Smaller ad-native players are in the $10–15M ARR range (Creatify, Arcads). **Foundation video models** are also monetizing quickly: Kling has a run rate of about $0.5B, Runway reports about $200M ARR, and MiniMax reported $116.6M revenue in H1 2026. Standalone **"performance creative" SaaS** is small or consolidating: AdCreative.ai was acquired by Appier, Pencil by Brandtech, and Omneky's revenue is about $2M. Meanwhile the ad platforms give generative creative away free inside their buying stacks.

### Inferences
- Avatar/UGC video has two revenue tiers:
  - Horizontal avatar platforms with $100M+ ARR (HeyGen, Synthesia)
  - Ad-specific UGC tools with about $10–15M ARR (Creatify, Arcads)
  - Ad-specific tools stay thin because the platforms (Meta, TikTok Symphony, Google) now bundle the same capability for free.
- The money in "performance creative" flows mainly to ad platforms, via better auction outcomes. Standalone creative SaaS has consolidated into holding companies and ad-tech firms (Pencil → Brandtech, AdCreative.ai → Appier).
- Template/code video (Remotion, Shotstack, Creatomate) is small, developer-tooling-sized. It is relevant mainly as the deterministic layer that wraps gen-AI shots (text, price, logo, resizing) — an inference.
